This book is about high-level knowledge: what options exist, how things work inside, and why to choose one approach over another. It is not about syntax. An agent can write the code; deciding what to build and why is still your job.

## How the book is organized

- **Concept pages** explain a problem and the possible solutions, independent of any tool.
- **Tool pages** are short. They link up to their concept and show the tool version they were checked against.
- **Quizzes** are separate. When a topic has one, you will see a "Check yourself" button at the end of the page.

Every page ends with links to the official docs and to related topics.
